# Business Requirements — QR Food System

---

## Problem Description

The university cafeteria currently processes meal payments manually. Group leaders record meals on paper, payments are made in cash, and monthly reports are created by hand. This leads to low student engagement — currently only 10–15 students use the cafeteria daily despite capacity for many more.

---

## Current Issues

| # | Issue | Impact |
|---|-------|--------|
| 1 | Meals recorded manually by group leaders | Human errors in records |
| 2 | Cash-only payments | Students often don't carry cash |
| 3 | Long queues at the counter | Students skip lunch to avoid waiting |
| 4 | Monthly reports created on paper | Accountant spends hours on manual data entry |
| 5 | No fast student identification | Cook cannot quickly find student data |

---

## Business Goal

Automate student meal accounting using QR codes and digital transactions to speed up service, reduce errors, and increase student engagement in cafeteria usage.

---

## Business Objectives

| # | Objective | Measurement |
|---|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | Speed up meal service | Order processed in under 1 minute |
| 2 | Eliminate manual meal recording | 0 paper records after system launch |
| 3 | Reduce payment errors | 0 balance errors per month |
| 4 | Automate monthly reporting | Report generated in 1 click |
| 5 | Increase student engagement | Growth from 10–15 to 50+ daily users |

---

## Expected Result

After system implementation students pay using QR codes in seconds, cooks process orders in 3 clicks, and accountants generate monthly reports automatically for any selected period instead of collecting data manually.

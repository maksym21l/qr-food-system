# Business Requirements

## Problem Description
Current student meal accounting is performed manually.

## Issues
- Group leaders record meals manually
- No fast student identification
- Monthly reports are created on paper

## Consequences
- Long queues
- Human errors
- Difficult accounting

## System Goal
The system must automate student meal accounting using QR codes and digital transactions.

## Objectives
- Speed up meal service
- Reduce manual work
- Reduce errors
- Generate automatic monthly reports
